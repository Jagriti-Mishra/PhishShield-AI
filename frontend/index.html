UNDERSTANDING THE THREAT

How Real-World Phishing Attacks Work (and How We Stop Them)

Modern phishing attacks use sophisticated visual cloning and newly registered domains to deceive users. Here is how PhishShield intercepts them at every stage.

⚠️

How Attackers Execute Lookalike Phishing

  1. 1
    Register a Lookalike Domain:

    Scammers purchase deceptive domains like sbi-online-kyc-update.top or paypal-secure-auth.xyz from bulletproof registrars.

  2. 2
    Clone Visual Look & Feel:

    They copy exact logos, brand color schemes, typography, and login input boxes so the page looks genuine to victims.

  3. 3
    Send Panic SMS or WhatsApp Link:

    Victims receive psychological panic bait: "URGENT: Your SBI Account will be blocked in 24 hours. Update KYC now at [Link]."

  4. 4
    Exfiltrate Credentials:

    When the victim enters credentials and OTPs, the hidden form secretly posts them to the attacker's unauthorized server.

🛡️

How PhishShield AI Automatically Protects You

  1. 1
    Automated Stream Ingestion:

    PhishShield continuously ingests newly registered domain (NRD) feeds 24/7 to catch threat infrastructure within hours of creation.

  2. 2
    Dual-Stage Computer Vision:

    Our visual AI matches the page layout against authentic brand templates using perceptual hashing & 128-D color gradients.

  3. 3
    DOM & Code AST X-Ray:

    We trace every form submission endpoint to catch credential theft endpoints and unencrypted HTTP transport.

  4. 4
    Instant Warning & Automated Takedown:

    Browsers receive instant red warning alerts, and registrars get an automated RFC 2142 abuse takedown notice to suspend the domain.
